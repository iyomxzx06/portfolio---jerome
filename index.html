About

Good editing is more than transitions. It's knowing what to keep, what to cut, and when to let a moment breathe.

I'm Jerome, and I'm building my craft through practice projects and creative experiments. I focus on short-form edits, YouTube ads, YouTube Shorts and AI-assisted animation and ads.

Every project here is a chance to test pacing, sound and storytelling ideas. I'm looking for opportunities to grow and collaborate.
